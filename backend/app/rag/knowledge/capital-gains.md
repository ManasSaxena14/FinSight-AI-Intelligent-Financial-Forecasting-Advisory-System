## Equity shares and equity mutual funds
- **Short-term** (held 12 months or less): taxed at 20%.
- **Long-term** (held more than 12 months): taxed at 12.5% on gains above ₹1.25 lakh in a financial year. The first ₹1.25 lakh of long-term equity gains each year is tax-free.

## Tax harvesting
Because the first ₹1.25 lakh of long-term equity gains is exempt every year, some investors sell and re-buy units to "book" gains within the exemption. Consider exit loads and transaction costs before doing this.

## Debt mutual funds
Gains on debt funds bought on or after 1 April 2023 are added to your income and taxed at your slab rate, regardless of how long you hold them.

## SIPs and holding period
For SIPs, each instalment has its own purchase date, so its holding period is counted separately. Units bought 11 months ago may still be short-term even if your first SIP started years earlier.

## Note
Tax rules on investments change frequently. Confirm current rates on incometax.gov.in or with a tax professional.
