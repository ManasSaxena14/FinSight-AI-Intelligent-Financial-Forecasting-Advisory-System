## Health insurance
Medical inflation in India is high, and one hospital stay can wipe out years of savings. An employer group policy may end when you change jobs, so many people also buy a personal or family-floater policy. Look at the sum insured, room-rent limits, co-payment, waiting periods for pre-existing diseases, and the claim-settlement record. A super top-up policy can add large cover cheaply. Premiums qualify under 80D in the old regime.

## Term life insurance
If anyone depends on your income, a pure term plan provides a large cover at low cost. A common guideline is cover of about 10–15 times your annual income, plus outstanding loans. Term plans pay only on death and have no maturity value, which is why they are cheap.

## Avoid mixing insurance and investment
Endowment and money-back policies usually give low returns and inadequate cover. Many people are better off with a term plan for protection and separate investments for growth.

## Other cover
Personal accident and critical-illness riders can be useful additions. Keep nominee details updated on every policy.
