## Inflation
The RBI targets consumer inflation of 4% within a band of 2–6%. Money that earns less than inflation after tax loses purchasing power, which is why long-term savings usually need some growth assets.

## Matching products to time horizons
- Under 1 year: savings account, sweep FD, RD, liquid fund.
- 1–3 years: FDs, short-duration debt funds.
- 5+ years: a mix that includes equity (index funds, EPF/PPF/NPS for retirement).

## Making a goal plan
1. Put a price and a date on the goal (adjust far-off prices for inflation).
2. Divide the remaining amount by the months left to get the monthly saving needed.
3. Check it against what you actually save — FinSight's goal planner simulates your real month-to-month savings to estimate the probability of reaching the goal on time.
4. If it doesn't fit, extend the date, lower the target, or cut a specific category.

## Children's goals
Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana is a government-backed scheme for a girl child under 10, with tax benefits under 80C (old regime) and maturity at 21 years. Education costs typically rise faster than general inflation.
