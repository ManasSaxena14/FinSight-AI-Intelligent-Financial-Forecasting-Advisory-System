## How recurring payments work
Subscriptions such as OTT apps, music, cloud storage and SIPs often use UPI AutoPay or card e-mandates. Under RBI rules, recurring debits above certain limits need extra authentication, and you get a pre-debit notification before each charge.

## Finding and cancelling mandates
Open your UPI app (for example the AutoPay or Mandates section) to see every active mandate, its amount and frequency. You can pause or revoke a mandate there. For card mandates, check your card's app or net banking.

## Subscription audit
Once a month, list recurring charges and cancel anything you haven't used in 30 days. Rotate OTT subscriptions instead of keeping several at once. Annual plans are cheaper only if you will really use the service for the full year.

## FinSight's recurring detection
FinSight looks for merchants that charge you a similar amount roughly every month and lists them as recurring, with the total monthly cost, so forgotten subscriptions stand out.
