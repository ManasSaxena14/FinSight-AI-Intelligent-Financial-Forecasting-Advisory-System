## What the score is
Credit scores in India (CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, CRIF High Mark) range from 300 to 900. Around 750 and above is generally considered good and helps you get loans at better rates.

## What moves it
- **Payment history** — paying EMIs and card bills on time matters most.
- **Credit utilisation** — keep card usage below about 30% of your total limit.
- **Credit mix and age** — a long history with both secured (home, car) and unsecured (cards, personal) credit helps; keep old cards open if they have no fee.
- **New enquiries** — many loan or card applications in a short time lower the score.

## Improving it
Set up autopay for at least the minimum due, then pay in full; reduce utilisation; avoid unnecessary applications; check your report for errors and raise disputes with the bureau. You are entitled to a free credit report from each bureau once a year.

## Recovery time
Late payments stay on the report for years, but their impact fades as you build a record of on-time payments.
